## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Current Refoss models (P11S plug, R11 and R21 relays, EM01P, EM06P and EM16P energy monitors) found on the local network through the mDNS discovery of Gladys, and connected over their local WebSocket: every change is pushed, no polling; a password set in the Refoss app is supported (SHA-256 digest).
- First Refoss models (R10 relay, EM06 and EM16 energy monitors) reached at the IP addresses typed in the configuration, over their local signed HTTP protocol, read on the refresh interval.
- One device per channel, identified by the MAC address of the box: outlets and relays as switches (with power, voltage, current and an energy index when the device measures them), shutter motors (R11 / R21 in cover mode) as shutters with their position once calibrated, current clamps as energy sensors (power, voltage, current, energy consumed and energy returned), named as in the Refoss app.
- Energy indexes that only go up, built from the counters of the devices (lifetime total, else this month) and kept in /data, for the energy monitoring of Gladys.
- Measurements sent on a refresh interval of 10, 30 or 60 seconds, on / off and shutters at once; a local or unreachable badge per device, automatic reconnection.
- Dashboard widgets "Refoss power" (every outlet and clamp, the hungriest first, live power and energy of the month) and "Refoss device" (live power, today, this month, 24-hour power curve, on / off or open / stop / close buttons).
- Scene triggers: a button wired on a device input is pressed (single, double, triple, long), a protection trips (overpower, overvoltage, overcurrent, undervoltage, overheating), a device is lost, a device is back.
- Scene actions: turn an outlet on for some minutes (kept across restarts), read the energy counters of a device.
- Configuration button "Search the devices".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
